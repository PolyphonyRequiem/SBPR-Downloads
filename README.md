[Download the Windows or Linux launcher](https://github.com/PolyphonyRequiem/SBPR-Downloads/releases/tag/prelude-playtest-0.2.45).

1. Install/update Valheim in Steam and sign in.
2. Download and extract the complete launcher archive for your OS.
3. Open `SBPR.Launcher.exe` (Windows) or `SBPR.Launcher` (Linux).
4. Confirm your Steam Valheim folder and select a new managed destination, review, consent and install.
5. Press Play; choose/create your character in Valheim, press Start, and enter the server password supplied privately by Daniel.

The UI's Prelude entry points to this trailborne-test playtest, not the production Prelude server. Trailborne is enabled, Stones is disabled. Your Steam installation and saves are not moved or reset. Existing characters retain their progression/inventory; the server world is fresh.
